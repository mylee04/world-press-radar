Understand the results.

Search the registered catalog by country, publisher name or domain, language, category, and endpoint type. The collection quality dashboard shows country coverage and source details.

Health results are cached and can become stale. Check times and content dates are separate; an enabled registration does not guarantee a working endpoint. Sitemap indexes describe child sitemap references; child files and article links are not validated by this service.

New URL counts describe first discoveries after an excluded initial baseline, not the number of articles published. Untracked periods and partial collection are identified in the results.

The plugin reads saved source metadata and observation aggregates. It does not search article contents, retrieve full articles, or fetch publisher sites during your request.
